DRAGONKIN TRAINER 1.1.1
Single-player / offline only. In a co-op session every cheat switches itself off.

REQUIREMENTS
  Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and Dragonkin: The Banished on Steam. Nothing else - no mod loader, no extra runtimes.

INSTALL
  Unzip this folder anywhere you like (not inside the game folder). Nothing is copied into the game.
  Keep the files in it together.

PLAY
  1. Run DragonkinTrainer.exe. It starts the game through Steam and loads the trainer by itself.
     (If the game is already running, it attaches to it.)
  2. Load your character and press INSERT to open the trainer. Change the key on the SETTINGS page.
  3. Cheats you switch on stay on after zone changes, deaths and restarts until you switch them off.
  Starting the game from Steam alone runs it without the trainer.

UNINSTALL
  Delete this folder.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Back up your saves before big changes: <game>\DragonkinTheBanished\Saved\SaveGames
  - trainer.log next to the trainer says what happened if something does not work.
  - TALENTS page: read what a talent does before you raise it - some talents can hurt you when used wrong.
  - ANCESTRAL GRID: the extra cells do not line up with the grid picture. If you turn the bigger grid off (or play
    without the trainer), fragments in extra cells go floating - they are kept, they just stop counting.
  - "Every fragment touches every fragment" and the grid size take effect after a zone change / loading your character.
